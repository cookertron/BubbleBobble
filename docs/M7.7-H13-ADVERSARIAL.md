## THE ONE ERROR THAT ACCOUNTS FOR MOST OF THIS

**TRAPS #43 bit the boss section in at least six places at once**, and every one
of them is a SELF-MODIFIED OPERAND -- precisely the class of byte a round-80 dump
cannot report.  The section's prose calls these blocks "byte-verified"; they were
verified against `build/image.bin`, which is a DUMP TAKEN MID-PLAY, where
`build/image_tape.bin` was the correct source:

## PER-SUBSYSTEM SUMMARIES

### entry (13 findings)

Every experiment below drove REAL rounds >= 2: an 81-round chained build (roundclass's recipe, warp suppressed, $745C := $AABB / $7E0E := $EE reseeded before every build) with rounds 16,17,18,72,73,79,80,81 instrumented; 300-1200 play frames on a chained round-80 world; 400 play frames each on chained rounds 79, 80 and 81; and 200/900 play frames on the shipped build/boss.pkl and build/bossspawn.pkl (both $8609 = $50).

HEADLINE: F0's "the boss is round 80" is NOT contradicted here -- the boss section already says round 80 is the fight and names round 81 the treasure room (SPEC:3099), and H6's staleness is consistent with the census ($7E0E = 3 on round 80, sentinel $EE survives on round 81). What IS wrong is almost everything the section says about how the round is SET UP, and the cause is one trap repeated six times.

TRAPS #43 bit this section in SIX places at once, all inside prose labelled "byte-verified" or "image default": $79A2 (LD A,10 vs tape LD A,5), $AA00 ($37 vs $2A), $AA48 ($FF vs $01), $A8C2 ($FE02 vs $5B00), $A85C (3 vs 1), $A8DB (24 vs 0). Two of the six are load-bearing (the HP seed and the frame gate), one is structurally wrong as a model ($AA00 is the SUB's own operand, not a scratch), and three are behaviourally inert but show the whole listing was verified against the wrong image.

THE ANSWER TO "which build stages run": F0's own fixture already contains it and the boss text never says. Across the 42-PC round-class signature, round 80 differs from round 79 in exactly four entries -- 79 has $72CA/$7336/$7373 (the enemy-spawn tail), 80 has $79A2 -- and EVERY other PC has an identical count. The boss round builds its collision map ($79AC x1), its tile and walls (H7's $840A x26 / $8447 x22, H8's $83EB x1), its element bank (H10's $BF39/$BF5B x1 each), its HUD furniture (H9's $806C/$957E/$9904) and runs the full scroll-in (H11's $7F80/$7F83/$7F97/$7FA9 x3 each, which round 81 loses two of). Nothing is skipped but the enemy spawner. Caveat stated: a PC-count signature is not a byte comparison.

It is READ by 25 sites, of which ELEVEN test it against $50. The boss text names three. The eight it omits include two behaviourally major ones: $903A CP $50 / RET z means THE BOSS ROUND HAS NO HURRY-UP, and $8A16 forces the power-up type to 13 -- which is the blue bottle, refuting the section's claim that the bottle is a fixed level object.

### lifecycle (14 findings)

Fourteen findings against SPEC.md's boss section, ten of them refutations. The headline: **the boss's frame gate `$A9FC` does not gate.** `$AA00` is the SUB's OWN OPERAND and `$AA01 LD ($AA00),A` writes the RESULT back, making it an involution s := (t - s) & $FF. Measured on boss.pkl: 202 entries at `$A9FC`, 202 passes at `$AA05`, over 20 frames. Its three siblings ($9E53/$A824/$A09A) use `CP` instead of `SUB` and therefore store the raw timer back, which IS the game's standard once-per-frame divider: each passed exactly 20 of 202 times in the same 20 frames. SPEC models the boss gate as "a 1-frame cosmetic stall, one frame per 256" with a CONSTANT `$37`; the constant is live state (12 distinct values in one 600-frame run) and there were ZERO stalls in 300 frames. When I armed the equality by hand, the stall skips about HALF of ONE frame's ticks and self-heals at the next frame boundary.

Consequence for the port: the ONLY divider the boss has is `$AA05`'s mod-32, and it counts MAIN-LOOP PASSES, not frames. 2890 passes / 32 = 90.3, and I measured exactly 90 movement bodies in those 300 frames. The pass rate is load-dependent (10.1/frame on boss.pkl, 8.8/frame on round1_48), so "one tile-step every 3.33 frames / 2.4 px/frame" is one world's number, not the rule.

On the assigned structural questions, SPEC is RIGHT and I confirmed it three independent ways: the boss is NOT an `$FD80` slot (slots 2..8 are all-zero in all four boss states, `$AA57`=9), it has NO `(IY+9)` -- its presence/state byte is `$A839`, 0 = absent -- and it does NOT use the shared pass or the shared walker (`$A9ED` has exactly ONE caller, `$8D2E`, by a full-image jump/call grep; `$B028` fired 0 times in 260 frames of round 80). But SPEC's lifecycle has no DESPAWN: I watched `$A839` go $10 -> $00 at frame 345 of a real 80->81 death, writer PC `$9855`, which is the 19-entry `$9AC1` pointer-table walk that every round advance runs. `$A839` is entry 12 of that table, and the death check's own gate1 byte `$8E3B` is entry 3.

And the boss is NOT frozen by H12's settler: from bossspawn.pkl it spawned at frame 38 with `$B54B` = 65 still counting, and ran 68 movement bodies before the entity pass stopped diverting at frame 167. A paired control (`$B54B` := 0) gave 48 bodies against 50 -- the settler touches the boss only through main-loop load, never through `$AA72`.

Three TRAPS #43 instances survive in SPEC because the section was written off build/image.bin: `$79A2` is `3E 05` on tape (seeds HP 10, not 20), `$AA48` is `$01` on tape (both velocities +1, not dy=-1), and `$9E57`/`$AA00` are live self-modified operands quoted as the constants `$58`/`$37`. No boss oracle exists yet (tools/bossroom.py is not in the tree), so nothing in this section has ever been gated.

### render (15 findings)

SPEC (b) models the boss blit as "one self-modified source operand $A8C2, 32x32 monochrome, 128 bytes, falls through into an attribute pass". Three of those four are wrong or incomplete.

### endgame (19 findings)

Nineteen findings; six of them refute documented claims outright.

THE HEADLINE: **the treasure room does not pre-place its diamonds.** A real F0 chained build to round 81 leaves the item block completely empty; one $89CB pass places exactly ONE item, type 12 at $9603 = $1C; the 25-ish diamonds exist only in $890D, handler 12's tail, which nothing in the 64K image calls.
