## The settled model

### === f: THE HURRY-UP ===

**f.1 -- The two counters, and why they are code.**  `$8DB5` is
`21 58 10 LD HL,nn / 23 INC HL / 22 B6 8D LD ($8DB6),HL / 21 58 10 LD HL,nn / 23 / 22 BD 8D
LD ($8DBD),HL / C9`, CALLed from `$8D81` inside the interrupt handler entered at `$8D72`.  **The
counters ARE the two `LD HL,nn` operands**: `$8DB6/$8DB7` is the round-elapsed frame counter and
`$8DBD/$8DBE` is the play/hurry timer, each +1 per interrupt = +1 per 69888 T frame.  They are
zeroed together at round start (`$988B`, `$988E`); only `$8DBD` is additionally zeroed by the
player-death chain (`$9170` inside `$915F`), the level clear (`$8EF9`), power-ups 10 and 11
(`$8898`) and the new game (`$96EC`).  Writers of `$8DBD` (`22 BD 8D`): six, exactly those plus the
ISR tick.  Writers of `$8DB6` (`22 B6 8D`): two, `$8DB9` and `$988E`.

`$8DBD`'s **low byte is the game's frame-edge clock**, not a private hurry variable.  `$9AEB`
(CALLed from `$8D58` on every chain pass) is `LD A,($8DBD) / LD HL,$A37C / CP (HL) / LD (HL),A /
RET z` -- do work only when the low byte changed.  There are **TEN** low-byte readers, not nine:
`$7E98, $8F2E, $91CC, $9AEB, $9E53, $A09A, $A190, $A829, $A9FC, $AB3F`, plus three high-byte readers
of `$8DBE` (`$892C, $8F29, $8F36`).  `$7E98` (`LD A,($8DBD) / CP $2A / RET nz`, reached from `$8D3A`
every chain pass) is a frame-exact one-shot that every hurry/death/level-clear reset re-arms.  So a
timer reset is not a private event -- it perturbs ten other routines.

**f.2 -- The per-round threshold `$745C`, and the two rounds that never get one.**  `$7373
LD ($745C),HL` is the only writer (`22 5C 74` -> 1 hit) and `$9041`/`$917D` (`2A 5C 74` -> 2 hits)
are the only readers, both 16-bit.  The selector at `$735D` is `CP $38 / LD HL,$15E / JR z / CP $48
/ LD HL,$7530 / JR z / LD HL,$3E8 / JR nc / LD HL,$5DC`.  Value table, measured by poking `$8609`
and driving `$735A`: rounds 1-55 and 57-71 = 1500 frames (29.95 s at the harness's 69888 T frame);
round 56 = 350; round 72 = 30000 (effectively disabled); rounds 73+ = 1000.

**BUT `$7373` DOES NOT RUN EVERY ROUND.**  `$729D LD A,($8609) / $72A0 CP $50 / $72A2 JP nc,$79A2`
diverts the spawner before it, and `$79A2` is `LD A,n / RLCA / LD ($79A3),A / LD ($9F62),A / RET`.
MEASURED BY ME with a sentinel no path can write (`$745C`:=$AABB) over whole `$97F8` builds with SP
pinned $96D7: round 79 -> `$745C`=$03E8 with `$7373` hit once and `$79A2` zero times; **rounds 80 and
81 -> `$745C` STILL $AABB, `$7373` hit ZERO times, `$79A2` hit ONCE**, and `$AA57` likewise stays at
the entry value ~~($05)~~ with `$72CA` hit zero times.  **CORRECTED 2026-09-17 (round-class): $05 is
`round1_48`'s entry value, an artefact of poking `$8609` into a round-1 world.  In REAL PLAY rounds
80 and 81 inherit `$AA57` = 9 -- on the sequential path (round 79 writes 9) AND on the milestone-warp
path (19 -> 81, where `$7373`/`$72CA` also never run).  The RANGE is 5..9 and the inherited value is
9; see F0.**  So the hurry threshold and the enemy count on
the two most special rounds in the game are INHERITED.  Round 80 does not care (`$903A CP $50 /
RET z`); **round 81 DOES run the hurry-up against a stale threshold**, which is 1000 arriving from
round 80 and 1500 arriving by the `$9846` milestone warp.  (`$727B`'s head still runs on 80/81 --
it clears `$FD80..$FDD9` and LDIRs `$95F3`->`$7581` -- so the rounds are enemy-free because the
table was wiped, not because "the spawner is skipped".)

`$79A3` is the OPERAND of its own `LD A,n` and self-doubles once per rounds-80/81 build.
build/image_tape.bin holds $05 there; build/image.bin (a round-80 dump) holds $0A -- I diffed the
two.  MEASURED: my round-80 build took it $05 -> $0A, and my round-81 build did the same.  Its
`$9F62` write is DEAD -- `$98B2 LD A,$2A / LD ($FEC5),A / LD ($9F62),A` runs later on every round,
and I measured `$9F62`=$2A after all six builds I ran.

**f.3 -- The trigger `$9037` and its five exits.**  One caller, `$8D4F CALL $9037`, in the main-loop
chain.  MEASURED BY ME from round1_48 (`$8DC4`==$C9 asserted, SP pinned $96D7, sentinel index 0 on
every row, `$745C` = 1500):

| world | T | after |
|---|---|---|
| round $50, timer 1501 | **31** | nothing latched, `$FEC4` untouched at $14 |
| timer 1500 (<= threshold) | **91** | nothing latched |
| `$9049`=1 (and `$9049`=$5A) | **107** | nothing latched |
| `$904D`=1, timer 2000 | **205** | `$9257` stays $00 |
| `$904D`=1, timer 2001 | **69,785** | `$9257`=$E0 |
| timer 1501, rounds $01/$4E/$4F/$51/$52 | **2,963,896** | `$904D`=$01, `$90AA`=$01, `$FEC4`=`$FEC5`=$01 |

The compare is `LD DE,($8DBD) / LD HL,($745C) / OR A / SBC HL,DE / RET nc`, so the trigger needs
timer >= threshold + 1: from a round start that is frame 1501 on a 1500-frame round.  The three
guards are three different things, all `LD A,n` OPERANDS with no `LD A,(nn)` reader:

* **`$9049`** (operand of `$9048`) = HURRY-UP SUPPRESSED FOR THIS ROUND.  Written 1 by exactly three
  routines -- `$88C8` (power-up 7, water), `$8929` (power-up 12, level skip), `$8EFD` (level clear)
  -- never written 0 by any store, cleared only by the round-start table.  One reader, `$9048`.
* **`$904D`** (operand of `$904C`) = ALREADY TRIGGERED.  Set at `$9059`, at the TOP of the sequence
  (not at `$911A`); cleared at `$916A` (`$915F`), `$ABA8` (player death) and by the round-start
  table.  Its second reader `$8DCB` is the 128K music selector and is DEAD on 48K, because the only
  entry `$8D8F CALL $8DC4` hits the `$C9` that `$969A` patched in at cold boot.
* **`$90AA`** (operand of `$90A9`) = THE ANGER PASS HAS RUN THIS ROUND.  It is NOT hurry-up-specific:
  `$90A9` has one CALL, `$8EC9`, reached when the live-enemy census `$8EAA` leaves exactly ONE enemy,
  and is ALSO fallen into from `$90A1`, the tail of `$9037`.

**f.4 -- The banner, and what it does to the machine.**  With all three guards clear the banner runs
INLINE and blocks the chain.  In order: `$9052` `$FEC5`:=0 and `$9055` `$FEC4`:=0 (BOTH ISR passes
off -- `$8DA2 LD A,($FEC4) / AND A / RET z / CALL $A3AD / JP $AA60` and `$8DAD LD A,($FEC5) / OR A /
RET z / JP $B62C`), `$9059` `$904D`:=1, `$905F` `$9A04`:=$FD2C, then three calls to `$90DA` with
B=10/11/1.  `$90DA`'s first act is `LD ($9102),A` -- **`$9102` is a self-modified OPCODE**, $00 =
NOP (erase+draw) and $C9 = RET (erase only) -- and only the third call patches it to $C9.  The text
is the 10-char string `"HURRY UP !"` at `$9A1C`, whose only user in the image is `$9110`.

The cursor `$9A04` walks DOWN from $FD2C by two per pass (`$90E9 LD IX,($9A04) / DEC IX / DEC IX /
LD ($9A04),IX`), and each pass reads IX+2/IX+3 as the DRAW row and IX+4/IX+5 as the ERASE row -- a
stride of TWO, so the same word is this pass's erase row and the next pass's draw row.  It is ONE
SLIDING ARRAY of screen addresses, not 22 two-byte entries.  The passes ACCELERATE (the per-pass
delay is B*256 inner iterations with B counting 10..1 then 11..1).  Between the two groups the
banner HOLDS at char row 13 for a 120-iteration loop that flashes ten attributes at `$59AB`.

Sound and colour, measured: 126 `OUT ($FE)` writes over the whole call, histogram {$10: 120,
$00: 6}, no other port touched, the $10 writes ~13,620 T apart at a constant level.  **There is no
klaxon and no border flash** -- the speaker line is held HIGH with THREE short dips and the border
bits are 0 in both values.  Three, because `$906A LD A,C / AND $1F / JR nz,$9083` fires only at
C = 96, 64, 32 out of C = 120..1.  Because three is ODD, the self-modified ink operand `$9077`
(XOR 5 per flash) does NOT return to where it started -- $03 on tape, $06 in the round-80 dump --
so consecutive hurry-ups start on the opposite ink.  `$9077` is live state.

Cost: **2,963,896 T = 42.41 frames** of pure blocking execution (phases 8.61 / 23.41 / 10.20 / 0.19
frames), and 44 frames of real main-loop stall in play, with the timer running 1501 -> 1545 across
it because interrupts stay enabled.

**THE BANNER INITIALISES BORIS.**  `$90DA`'s `DJNZ` at `$9118` FALLS THROUGH into `$911A`, so
"returning" from a NOPed pass-group also zeroes `$918B`/`$9194` and writes `$9259`:=8, `$9258`:=0,
`$9255`:=$18, `$9256`:=$A8, `$9254`:=2, and patches the two target operands `$91F8`:=the chosen
player's Y address and `$921C`:=that player's X address.  **Two of the three calls are NOPed, so
`$911A` runs TWICE per trigger** -- it is the SECOND run that decides which player Boris chases.

Then `$90A1`/`$90A6` restore both gates to the LITERAL 1 (not to their previous values -- `$FEC4`
goes $14 or $58 -> $01 permanently, invisible to its only reader's `AND A` but visible to a memory
gate), and if `$90AA` was 0 it is set and `$90C1` runs with `$FEC4` held at 0 again.

**f.5 -- The sweep `$90C1`, and the opcode byte the port has no model of.**  MEASURED BY ME from
round1_48 with every slot seeded DISTINCT and write-visible (+7 := `$80|(slot&3)` with bit 2 CLEAR,
+5 := `$10*slot+7` with the low three bits SET, +9 := $E7 as an untouched sentinel), SP pinned
$96D7, sentinel index 0, ordered log over `$FD00-$FEFF`:

```
$AA57= 5 -> 3 iterations   SET2 @ FD9B FDA5 FDAF                     diffs [10,10]
$AA57= 9 -> 7 iterations   SET2 @ FD9B FDA5 FDAF FDB9 FDC3 FDCD FDD7 diffs all 10
$AA57=14 -> 12 iterations  SET2 @ FD9B .. FE09                       diffs all 10
AND-$F8 @ exactly 2 below each SET; (addr-$FD80) mod 10 == 5 for every one of them
writer PCs: ONLY $90CF and $90D5.  +9 sentinels still $E7 in every slot.
```

**THE STRIDE IS TEN** -- the `LD DE,12` at `$90CA` is cancelled by the two `DEC HL` that precede the
`ADD HL,DE` -- and **the masked field is +5 (X), not +9**.  The count is `($AA57)-2` with NO zero
test.  Three independent sites corroborate the stride with a bare `LD DE,10`: `$8EB3` (the
level-clear tally, same base `$FD9B`), `$AAEF` (the master update pass, base `$FD80`, the routine
that actually drives the table every frame) and `$7E9E`.

**`$90CF` is `CB D6` = `SET 2,(HL)`, and its second byte `$90D0` is a LIVE SELF-MODIFIED OPCODE.**
`$ABD5` is `LD A,($90D0) / XOR $40 / LD ($90D0),A`, and `$D6 ^ $40 = $96 = RES 2,(HL)`.  The player
respawn brackets its own call: `$ABB4 CALL $ABD5 / $ABB7 CALL $90C1 / $ABBA CALL $ABD5`.  So the
hurry-up ANGERS every enemy and **a player respawn CALMS every enemy** -- and both snap X to the
8-px grid.  `CD C1 90` has exactly two hits (`$90B5`, `$ABB7`); `$90D0` has exactly one reader and
one writer, both inside `$ABD5`; `$90D0` is $D6 in every 48K saved state and in both images.  A port
that hard-codes SET leaves the board permanently angry after a death.

The sweep is BLIND to bit 7 -- it angers dead slots too -- and the X snap is TRANSIENT (`$AB0C` does
`LD A,(IY+5) / AND $FE` on every pass and the walker moves X).  What bit 2 actually buys: `$B02E
LD A,$16 / BIT 2,(IY+7) / JR z / ADD A,A / LD ($B60F),A`, and `$B60F`'s only other reference is
`$B0A2 LD HL,$B60F / LD A,(IY+4) / SUB (HL) / JP nc,$B1B7`.  It is the HOP-CONSIDERATION WINDOW, not
a speed: angry doubles the slice of the 256-tick cycle in which an enemy may climb, 22/256 -> 44/256.
The walk step is `($B610)`, set to 2 once per round at `$9874`, which bit 2 never touches.

**f.6 -- The window, and the retire path.**  The `$FEC4`-off window around the sweep SCALES with
`$AA57`: 300 T at `$AA57`=5 (round 1) rising to 576 T at 9, i.e. 0.43%-0.82% of a frame, once per
round.  Retirement is `$915F` (called from `$8D34` every chain pass), whose operand `$9160` IS the
request and fires only on the value exactly 1 (`$9161 DEC A / RET nz`).  `$8ED4` (level clear) and
`$ABB1` (player respawn) set it.  It does `DI`, clears `$9160`/`$9257`/`$904D`, zeroes the 16-bit
`$8DBD`, sets `IY=$9250`, `CALL $A314`, `EI`.

### === g: BORIS (Baron von Blubba) ===

**g.1 -- One record, three pointers, no slot.**  Boris is TEN bytes at `$9250..$9259` laid out
exactly like an entity: +0/+1 sprite pointer, +2 cached Y, +3 cached X, +4 stage, +5 X, +6 Y,
+7 flags, +8, +9.  `FD 21 50 92` (`LD IY,$9250`) has exactly THREE hits in the whole 64K -- `$9121`,
`$9173`, `$918D` -- and `$9257` is referenced only at `$9168`, `$92E0` and `$9ACB` (entry 5 of the
`$9AC1` round-start zero table).  There is ONE Boris and there can never be a second.  Nothing in
`$917C..$924F` touches `$FD80-$FDF7`.

The cache order is settled by the CONSUMER, not by a snapshot: `$A600 LD A,(IY+5) / LD (IY+3),A` and
`$A620 LD A,(IY+6) / LD (IY+2),A`, so +3 is X and +2 is Y.  I read both images: tape struct =
`50 FF 00 00 01 18 A0 00 00 08`, live round-80 = `50 FF A8 88 04 88 A8 00 00 08`.

**g.2 -- Activation.**  `$917C` has ZERO CALL or JP references; its single entry is the tail jump
`$904F C2 7C 91 JP nz,$917C` from `$9037` (grepbytes: `CD 7C 91` and `C3 7C 91` -> 0 hits each,
`C2 7C 91` -> 1 hit at `$904F`).  Its gate is `LD HL,($745C) / LD DE,$1F4 / ADD HL,DE /
LD DE,($8DBD) / SBC HL,DE / RET nc`, so activation is timer >= threshold + **$1F5 = 501**, not 500.
MEASURED BY ME through `$9037` with `$904D` pre-set: timer 2000 -> T=205 and `$9257` stays $00;
timer 2001 -> T=69,785 and `$9257`=$E0.  (A direct `$917C` call stopped at `$91D4` shows `$80` --
bit 7 from `$91A3` -- and the other two bits arrive from the BLITTER, `$A45A SET 6` and `$A5E0
SET 5`.  Bit 6 is the FIRST-DRAW gate: `$A45E JP z,$A5D9` skips the erase while it is clear, so a
port that only models `$91A3`'s SET 7 will erase from a stale cache on Boris's first drawn frame.)
`$9187 SBC HL,DE` has NO `OR A` before it -- it relies on `$9182 ADD HL,DE` leaving carry clear,
which is safe only because `$7373` can never write a threshold above $7530.

**g.3 -- Spawn position and target.**  `$912C LD (IY+5),$18` and `$9130 LD (IY+6),$A8`: Boris always

Standing rules for every oracle below, none of which is restated per increment:
* **PIN SP PER CALL** and assert the sentinel index is 0.  `Harness.call` pushes six sentinels and
  never restores SP; it drifts 10 bytes per call and a long sweep walks the stack into ROM and
  silently records "nothing happened" (TRAPS #42).  With six sentinels a clean return leaves SP at
  `pinned-10`, not `pinned+2` -- assert that.  In this area there are two sharper instances:
  build/r6_48.pkl and build/r30_48.pkl carry SP = `$FD2E`, **inside the row tables**, so driving
  `$7F1B` from them without repinning corrupts `$FD04` mid-scroll; and **a routine that LDIRs over
  its own caller's SP destroys the sentinels** (`$96A5`, H0), so pin SP OUTSIDE the routine's write
  range, not merely to a fixed value.  Every H oracle must also assert `$FD00-$FD7F` is unchanged
  afterwards.
* **ASSERT `m[$8DC4] == $C9`** before trusting any state (TRAPS #28/#36).  build/title.pkl and

  write list checks nothing (TRAPS #44).  And seed with a value the routine cannot write: seeding
  `$47` hid a whole `$79AC` writer for this entire pass.
* **EVERY GATE MUST DRIVE AT LEAST ONE ROUND >= 2 AND MUST SAY WHICH ROUNDS IT DROVE** -- see the
  standing rule at the top of this document.  Round 1 collapses at least ten independent rules at
  once and it is the round every fixture in this repository is captured from.
* **NO FIXED T PHASE, ANYWHERE.**  Every timing-related figure comes from tools/capture.py over a
  varied phase, and every instruction stamp is an ordered inequality, never a point.
* **A NEGATIVE CARRIES ITS WINDOW.**  "Nothing writes X" is not a finding until the gate says over
